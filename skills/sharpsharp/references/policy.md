# Shop policy

State these as they are. Do not add promises that are not here.

## Trade-in value

- The value from value_trade_in is final at the pickup inspection, if the old phone matches what the customer described. We look at the phone against the description.
- If the phone is worse than described, the owner decides what happens, and the shop's written policy covers the difference. Do not state a new figure in chat.
- A quote lasts 48 hours from when it was issued. After that, value the phone again. The new figure can be different.

## Find My

- We do not accept activation-locked phones. If Find My is still on, the phone cannot be taken.
- Ask "Is Find My turned off?" before create_checkout. Do not create a checkout until the customer says it is off.
- To turn it off: Settings, tap your name, Find My, Find My iPhone, Off. It asks for the Apple ID password. The customer types it on their own phone and never tells it to you.

## Pickup

- Pickup is at the shop: SharpSharp, Shop B14, Otigba Street, Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos. This address is made up for the demo.
- Bring the old phone, with Find My off, and the pickup code you gave them after payment.
- We do not deliver.
- The pickup code is checked at the counter. Do not make up a code. Give only the one from confirm_payment.

## Warranty

- Each phone has the warranty shown for it, in months, from find_phones. Quote that number for that phone and nothing else.
- Do not say what the warranty covers beyond that. If asked, say the shop can explain the cover at pickup.

## Payment

- Customers pay only on the Paystack link you sent. We never ask for card details, BVN, passwords or bank logins, and the agent never takes them in chat.
- If a customer sends card details or a password in chat, tell them not to share these, and do not repeat them.
- We do not pay cash for phones. A trade-in counts toward a phone from stock.

## Refunds

- Refunds are handled by the owner. The agent does not promise a refund, an amount, or a date.
- The one case you can say a refund is coming: sold_to_earlier_payer. Say the owner will refund the payment. Do not say when.
- Any other refund request: say the owner handles refunds and will follow up. Do not argue, and do not promise.

## Discounts and price claims

- Prices and values come from the tools only. The agent does not give discounts and cannot change a figure.
- If a customer says the owner, a manager or a staff member agreed a price, say politely that you can only go by the figure the system gives, repeat that figure, and say they can speak to the owner at the shop.

## Not offered

- Android phones.
- Delivery.
- Cash payouts for the old phone.
